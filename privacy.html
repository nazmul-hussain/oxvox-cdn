oxvox Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

oxvox is an English learning app. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how you can delete it.

Data we collect

  • Account (optional): name, username, email address and/or phone number, and a password (stored only as a secure hash). You only need an account to sync your progress across devices.
  • Learning data: word progress and review schedule, daily activity (words studied, lessons, points), streak, completed study days, quiz results, missed words, bookmarks, listening results, level test results, and settings (levels, plan length, theme, reminder times).
  • Device: notification permission (for study reminders). Reminders are scheduled on your device.

Without an account, all learning data stays only on your device.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your learning data in sync between the app and the website.
  • To send study reminders you have turned on, such as a streak reminder or a reminder to review missed words.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties for marketing.

Where data is stored and how it is sent

Account and learning data is stored on our servers (MongoDB Atlas) and sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Learning content such as word lists and listening lessons is downloaded from a public content server and contains no personal information.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: open the Account panel, choose Delete account, and enter your password. Your login and learning data are removed from our servers, and the data on your device is cleared.
  • Without the app, email us (below) to request deletion.

Signing out clears the learning data on that device. Your data stays on the server until you sign in again or delete the account.

Third-party links

Community buttons open WhatsApp and Facebook groups. Their privacy policies apply on those services.

Children

oxvox is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes and show the new date above.
