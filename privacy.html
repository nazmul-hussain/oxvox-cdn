oxvox Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

oxvox is an English learning app. We respect your privacy.

Data we store

All your learning data (progress, streak, XP, badges, settings, display name, reminder time) is stored only on your device. We do not collect, sell, or share it.

Internet access

The app downloads public learning content (word lists and listening lessons) from a public content server. These requests do not include personal information.

Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder, the app asks for notification permission. You can turn it off at any time in the app or in Android settings.

Third-party links

Community buttons open WhatsApp and Facebook groups. Their privacy policies apply on those services.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
